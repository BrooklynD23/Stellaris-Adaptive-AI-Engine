## CI gates (resource proposal §8 + `signoff_fable.md`)

| Gate | Where | What it proves |
|---|---|---|
| CI-1 | `tests/e2e/test_ci1.py` | `sae synth --record` 30 y, 5 schedules × seeds (2 by default, 4 with `T7_E2E_FULL=1`), then `sae replay` of each recording at forced H0 vs H2: pooled one-to-one switch agreement ≥ 0.90 within a 24-month window, ≥ 8 switches. A same-seed digest test runs first. |
| CI-2 | `tests/integration/test_ci2.py` | Skipped with a reason: the opportunistic queue is cut (C7 D-13). |
| CI-3 | T5's `tests/unit/t5/test_governor*.py`, named step in `ci.yml` | Not duplicated. The CI step fails if its `-k` selection is empty. |
| CI-4 | `tests/integration/test_ci4_{imports,footprint,manifest}.py` | Fresh process after a real synth run: the `uv.lock` runtime closure is pinned exactly and every module is stdlib or in it; no rerun/duckdb/GPU modules and no `sae.telemetry`/`bench`/`m0`; peak RSS ≤ 150 MiB (measured 46 MiB); no GPU package anywhere in the lock (extras included). |
| CI-5 | `tests/e2e/test_ci5.py` | Every fixture parses with `cost` fields. A replayed session with a 256 MiB-padded save under a 128 MiB worker cap gives `health budget_breach`, observations continue and the run exits 0. Linux only: RLIMIT_AS, or a systemd scope where delegated. |
